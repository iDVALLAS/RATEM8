1. **Patch log:** the briefs want `PATCHLOG.md`; the repo's log is
   `LoanM8_Project_Handoff_README.md` (v1–v30). C/L patches go in
   `PATCHLOG.md`, with a pointer from the old log.

   `check:identity` allows names and numbers only there. C2 creates
   `config/licensing.ts` as the brief says, makes `lib/config.ts` derive
   from it, and adds it to the allowlist.

   `lib/prompts/m8-system.ts`. C3 replaces it there.

     into by hand (`arive_local`).

     TOTP (`arive_rpa`), on paid hosting.
   - The project's earlier rule was "Do not build automated logins to lender
     portals". The owner has to pick one before L4b Phase 0.

   `MloText` `{first}` template, so names stay in config.
